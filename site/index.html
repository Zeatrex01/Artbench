The art side of your game, in one place.

Artbench reads your Unity project, and the art folder you keep beside it: what each asset is, what uses it, what nothing uses, and what changed since you last looked. It runs on your computer.

In Blender

Notes that sit on the model

The Artbench add-on pins notes and to-dos to your objects in Blender's 3D view. They're kept inside the .blend and work without Artbench. When it's running, one click sends them to its task board.

Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions › Install from Disk: artbench_bridge.zip, in the download or on its own.

Your AI tools

Ask in words, get the list

Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Codex can use Artbench over MCP: what nothing uses, what changed, what a material ends up in. They can add a task to the board too. The Projects screen adds Artbench to Claude Code in one click.

claudeartbench over MCP

The rule

Nothing is overwritten silently

  1. Artbench writes nothing into your Unity project on its own.
  2. Every send or pull shows a plan, file by file, and waits for your OK.
  3. A file changed on both sides is a conflict that waits for your decision.
  4. Replaced copies go to an archive. Nothing is deleted.

Install

Three steps, no installer

  1. 1
    Download Artbench {{VERSION}} for Windows and unzip it anywhere, but not inside a game project.
  2. 2
    Double-click Artbench.bat. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. 3
    Open Projects and pick your Unity project, and your art folder if you have one.

Optional

Blender 4.2 or newer adds model previews, triangle counts and what changed inside a model.

The Blender add-on is in the download (blender_addon\artbench_bridge.zip), or on its own.

Good to know

Usage and references need the Unity project saved as text (Unity's default, Force Text).

Settings stay in the Artbench folder. To remove it, delete the folder; nothing else is installed.
